01 / THE JOURNEY

From innovation to impact

The long view

Milestones on the road to a more sustainable future.

  1. 1968

    The beginning

    Intel is founded

    Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore establish Intel, setting a course for decades of technology innovation.

    Explore milestone

    What starts as a semiconductor company will help make computing smaller, more capable, and more widely available.

  2. 1971

    A new era of computing

    The first microprocessor

    Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor.

    Explore milestone

    Putting a computer's central processing functions on a single chip opens the door to a new generation of devices.

  3. 1978

    Built to scale

    The 8086 processor

    The 8086 processor lays the foundation for the x86 architecture.

    Explore milestone

    Its instruction set becomes a lasting part of the computing landscape, powering generations of personal computers and servers.

  4. 1985

    More capability

    The 386 processor

    Intel introduces its first 32-bit processor, expanding what personal computers can do.

    Explore milestone

    The 386 brings new performance and multitasking capabilities to personal computing.

  5. 2006

    A turning point

    Emissions reach a peak

    Intel's annual operational greenhouse gas emissions reach their highest point.

    Explore milestone

    In the years that follow, Intel invests in chemical abatement, renewable energy, and more energy-efficient manufacturing.

  6. 2020

    A shared direction

    RISE goals for 2030

    Intel launches its RISE strategy: Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling.

    Explore the 2030 goals

    The goals focus on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction, while aiming to encourage progress across the industry.

  7. 2022

    The long-term ambition

    Net-zero by 2040

    Intel commits to net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations.

    Explore the commitment

    The commitment covers Scope 1 and 2 emissions and builds on years of environmental initiatives.

  8. 2023

    Powering progress

    99% renewable electricity

    Intel reports 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide.

    Explore milestone

    Renewable electricity is one part of the work to reduce operational emissions and advance long-term goals.

  9. 2024

    Working together

    First Sustainability Summit

    Intel brings suppliers, government, and industry leaders together to discuss sustainable manufacturing.

    Explore milestone

    Collaboration helps tackle shared challenges across the semiconductor supply chain.

The work behind the goals

02

Advance water stewardship through conservation, restoration, and responsible use.

03
